About Habesha Eatery

Welcome to Habesha Eatery

Habesha Eatery is a restaurant dedicated to bringing the rich flavors and traditions of Ethiopian cuisine to our customers. We prepare our dishes using fresh ingredients and traditional recipes to give you an authentic Ethiopian dining experience.

Our Food

Our menu includes a variety of Ethiopian dishes, appetizers, main courses, and refreshing drinks. You can enjoy traditional favorites such as Sambusa, Chechebsa, Doro Wot, Tibs, Kitfo, Ethiopian Coffee, Mango Juice, and Papaya Juice . Each dish is prepared with carefully selected ingredients and traditional Ethiopian flavors. We aim to provide delicious food that represents the culture and taste of Ethiopia.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide our customers with delicious Ethiopian food, friendly service, and a comfortable dining experience. We want every customer to feel welcome and enjoy the traditional flavors of Ethiopia.

Why Choose Habesha Eatery?

  • Authentic Ethiopian cuisine
  • Fresh and carefully selected ingredients
  • Traditional recipes and flavors
  • A variety of appetizers, main dishes, and drinks
  • Warm and welcoming service
  • A comfortable place to enjoy Ethiopian food

Visit Us

Habesha Eatery is located in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia . Whether you are looking for a traditional Ethiopian meal, a refreshing drink, or a warm cup of Ethiopian coffee, we welcome you to dine with us.

Thank you for choosing Habesha Eatery. We look forward to serving you!
